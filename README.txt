SkillRat - level your professions for the fewest coins (WoW Forever)

- Auction House: SkillRat tab. "Scan prices" scans the reagents of the selected profession,
  "Scan whole AH" scans everything (once every 15 minutes).
- Pick a profession, a minimum and a maximum level, then "Create the cheapest list".
- Click a craft to show its reagents in the Auction House; "Search all" for the whole list.
- "Pin" keeps the list on screen. /skillrat opens SkillRat anywhere.
- Open your profession window once so SkillRat knows your learned recipes.

Recipe data: AtlasLoot Classic by sliccer and contributors (GPL v2). Skill-up levels: WoW Forever game data (SkillLineAbility, via wago.tools). SkillRat is GPL v2 (see LICENSE).
