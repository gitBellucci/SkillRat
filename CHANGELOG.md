# 1.0.2

- **Reagents now come from WoW Forever's own game data**: about 130 recipes need different reagents on Forever than in Classic (Minor Mana Potion: Mageroyal and Peacebloom). Thanks for the report!
- **"Trainer + known recipes" no longer suggests recipes you have to buy or find**: SkillRat now reads from the game which recipes are learned from a recipe item. Those only show with "All recipes", or once you know them.

# 1.0.1

- **Skill-up levels now come from WoW Forever's own game data**: the ~1,500 recipes Forever added or changed (First Aid potions, rebalanced Leatherworking...) now give the right number of crafts. Recreate your lists.
- The bottom of the list reads like a quote: Auction House price, what the crafts sell for at a vendor, and the total in green (you make money) or red (what the leveling costs you).
- Reagents in clear columns: quantity, name, source (Auction House or vendor), what you already have in your bags, cost.
- **Save** your lists and open them again later from **Lists**, on any character of your account, priced with the prices of the day.
- Bigger, easier to read list text.

# 1.0.0

- First release.
- **Cheapest craft list**: pick a profession, a minimum and a maximum level, and SkillRat builds the cheapest list of crafts to get there from current Auction House prices and quantities (orange, yellow and green skill-up chances included), with how many to craft and the total cost.
- **SkillRat tab in the Auction House**: scan the reagents of one profession (every listing with its quantity) or the whole Auction House (once every 15 minutes).
- **Next to your profession window**: a movable SkillRat panel opens with it, your learned professions at the top.
- Exclude reagents you don't want to use; choose trainer + known recipes, known only, or all.
- Click a craft to show its reagents in the Auction House (all at once with Auctionator); "Search all" for the whole list.
- Reagents show what you already have in your bags: "you have it" in green, or "you have 12/52".
- **Save** your lists and open them again later, on any character of your account (**Lists**), priced with the prices of the day.
- Pin the list to keep it on screen; its "Search AH" button shows only the list's reagents in the Auction House.
- The bottom of the list reads like a quote: Auction House price, what the crafts sell for at a vendor, and the total in green (you make money) or red (what the leveling costs you).
- Scan progress bar with a Stop button.
- Vendor reagents use vendor prices, updated from the vendors you visit.
- Skill-up levels from WoW Forever's own game data (the ~1,500 recipes Forever added or changed included); recipes and reagents from AtlasLoot Classic (GPL v2).
